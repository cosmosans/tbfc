Change log

THU NOV 26 2026

  • STREET has a new home at The Gulch: a compact railway-side court with mini goals, fencing on all four sides, and passing freight trains. Play keeper-free 3v3 or 5v5, choose from nine court colours, and set the lighting and weather. Cabbagetown remains available in Classic.

NOV 25 2026

  • Kick Off now opens a game-mode grid with Classic and the new Street mode. Play keeper-free 3v3 or 5v5 at Cabbagetown with smaller goals, wall rebounds, eight court colours, and four formations for each team size. Build your squad with chemistry ratings and Balanced Auto Pick, choose timed matches or First to Five, and jump straight into a rematch from the results screen.
  • West End’s turf now has a consistent texture across the pitch and less glare under the station lights.
  • Outdoor matches now play differently in Rain and Snow. Rain slows the ball by 30% across the pitch, and three randomly placed puddles take 90% of its speed per second. In Snow, frost builds to full cover over 20 match minutes. Players and the ball leave tracks, ground friction drops by 10%, and the bright orange ball stays visible even in the keeper’s hands.
  • Career seasons now randomize weather across all eight matchweeks: winter brings at least two snow games, including one at night; spring has at least three rainy games; summer has at least one; and fall stays rain-free. Snow only appears in winter.
  • Fixed the Goal Dancers women’s running goal celebration so players keep their normal size in both kits.
  • Visit Debug → Match Intros to preview any field by day, in the evening, or at night. Press Esc during a preview to pick another.
  • All six fields now open Kick Off and Career matches with their own cinematic intro.
  • Fixed an intermittent black screen when starting the Old 4th Ward intro.
  • Look for your home crest on the Candler Park chase car and parked cars on the way up to Midtown’s rooftop pitch.
