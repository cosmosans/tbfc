Change log

FRI NOV 27 2026

  • HOUSE RULES completes the twelve-mode Kick Off grid. Build your own Classic 7v7 or keeperless Street 3v3/5v5 match with timed, target-score, or Golden Goal rules. Choose Standard, Long Range, or Air Balls scoring, then mix compatible Burn, stamina, tackling, Survival, goalkeeper, shooting, sprint, and physics options. A free-moving controller cursor, clickable option buttons, LB/RB tab switching, eight ready-made presets, saved custom rules, and Randomize make experimenting easy. Review your rules before kickoff and while paused, then rematch or edit them from the results screen.
  • AIR BALLS joins Kick Off at all six regular stadiums. Score with headers, volleys, half-volleys and direct set pieces; grounded open-play goals earn no point and give the defenders a goal kick. More forgiving aerial contact, buffered shooting and assisted crosses help set up first-time finishes, with extra accuracy for aerial shots and technique-specific goal callouts. Keep it up and finish in style.
  • MYSTERY MATCH joins Kick Off. Play a regular 7v7 match at any of the six main stadiums while one surprise modifier changes the action every 60 seconds of active play. Fire, ice, double points, unusual ball physics and more affect both teams; a compact display shows the current rule and countdown, and the results recap each change.
  • Keepers automatically punt after holding the ball for four real-world seconds to keep play moving. The holding timer stops while the match is paused.
  • BEAT THE CLOCK joins Kick Off as a solo or same-team co-op challenge against the CPU at the regular stadiums. Start with 90 seconds: every goal credited to your team adds 30 seconds, while every goal you concede costs 15. Pick either side, chase a separate solo or co-op personal best for your difficulty, and keep playing if a co-op partner disconnects. The challenge pauses for a reconnect if everyone disconnects.
  • SURVIVAL joins Kick Off with its own illustrated card and the familiar timed 7v7 rules at all six regular stadiums. Every goal costs the team awarded the point one randomly chosen active outfield player—even on an own goal. The reveal comes after the celebration and replay, then the remaining teammates regroup for kickoff. Each side can lose up to four players, while its goalkeeper and at least two outfield players stay on the pitch. A new strip below the score shows both teams’ remaining players at a glance. Eliminations carry through halftime, the results recap who was lost and when, and Rematch brings both full squads back. Score a goal. Lose a teammate. Winning has consequences.
  • PENALTIES joins Kick Off with 3, 5, or 7 scheduled kicks and sudden death when needed. Play at any of the six regular stadiums, choose match conditions in the familiar settings panel, and take turns through each full squad. Sean has new random goalkeeper warm-up animations, and the results show every kick.
  • Kick Off now shows four game modes per row, with smaller card titles so SNOWPOCALYPSE fits. Controller legends on the setup screens match the main menu size.
  • All six Kick Off modes now have their own illustrated menu artwork: Classic, Street (Beta), Snowpocalypse, Long Range, First to 5, and Inferno.
  • Burn shots now travel 15% slower in every mode, giving players and goalkeepers a little more time to react while keeping the full Burn shot spectacle.
  • Settings now includes a Controls screen with an illustrated controller guide. Press B to return to Settings.
  • INFERNO joins Kick Off as a classic 7v7 match with unlimited Burn for both teams. Every player starts on fire, Burn shots stay ready after goals and restarts, and a subtle orange glow marks players on the pitch. The usual match rules, stadiums, weather, saves, and replays still apply.
  • FIRST TO 5 joins Kick Off as an untimed 7v7 match with no halftime. Choose first to 3, 5, or 10 goals in Match Settings; the scoreboard tracks each goal, and the deciding strike ends the match after its celebration and replay.
  • Own goals now show the awarded team gathering in a circle for their group celebrations while the conceding team stands idle and the camera follows the celebrating team.
  • The Gulch has refreshed concrete, weathered metal, wood, and gym-tile court materials. Court colour choices now tint the tiles while preserving their seams and surface finish.
  • The referee has new idle, running, and yellow-card animations, with a consistent size alongside the players. He holds the final pose with the yellow card extended.
  • LONG RANGE joins Kick Off as a 7v7 mode where goals struck from outside the dotted scoring box earn two points. The line appears at both ends of the pitch, long shots have more bite, and open shooters get more attention from the AI. Land a two-point goal for a DOUBLE POINTS! celebration.
  • SNOWPOCALYPSE brings extreme winter 7v7 to Kick Off, with a fully snow-covered pitch, slippery movement, faster ball travel, longer slide tackles and a bright orange ball. Snow now also builds across surrounding outdoor ground, rooftops and treetops, with softer edges and a gradual fade into the distance—even in regular snowy matches. Atlanta shuts down. Football doesn't.

THU NOV 26 2026

  • The main menu now has borderless options and a spinning soccer ball beside your selection. Controller status appears below the Move and Select legend. The logo on the start screen and main menu shines with a two-second shimmer sweep every four seconds.
  • The title screen now shows a small “Press any button to continue” prompt beneath the logo. Use any controller button or keyboard key to enter the main menu.
  • The Gulch now has detailed warehouse buildings beyond both ends of the court, closing off the empty horizon with an industrial backdrop.
  • Two seconds after a goal, press A (J on keyboard) to skip the celebration and go straight to the replay.
  • Menus now share centered controller legends with consistent type, clear button labels, transparent actions, and fewer duplicate navigation buttons.
  • STREET has a new home at The Gulch: a compact railway-side court with mini goals, fencing on all four sides, and passing freight trains. Play keeper-free 3v3 or 5v5, choose from nine court colours, and set the lighting and weather. Cabbagetown remains available in Classic.

NOV 25 2026

  • Kick Off shows Classic, Street (Beta), and Snowpocalypse in one centered row. The highlight follows your selection, lighting up one mode at a time. Play keeper-free 3v3 or 5v5 at Cabbagetown with smaller goals, wall rebounds, eight court colours, and four formations for each team size. Build your squad with chemistry ratings and Balanced Auto Pick, choose timed matches or First to Five, and jump straight into a rematch from the results screen.
  • West End’s turf now has a consistent texture across the pitch and less glare under the station lights.
  • Outdoor matches now play differently in Rain and Snow. Rain slows the ball by 30% across the pitch, and three randomly placed puddles take 90% of its speed per second. In Snow, frost builds to full cover over 20 match minutes. Players and the ball leave tracks, ground friction drops by 10%, and the bright orange ball stays visible even in the keeper’s hands.
  • Career seasons now randomize weather across all eight matchweeks: winter brings at least two snow games, including one at night; spring has at least three rainy games; summer has at least one; and fall stays rain-free. Snow only appears in winter.
  • Fixed the Goal Dancers women’s running goal celebration so players keep their normal size in both kits.
  • Visit Debug → Match Intros to preview any field by day, in the evening, or at night. Press Esc during a preview to pick another.
  • All six fields now open Kick Off and Career matches with their own cinematic intro.
  • Fixed an intermittent black screen when starting the Old 4th Ward intro.
  • Look for your home crest on the Candler Park chase car and parked cars on the way up to Midtown’s rooftop pitch.
